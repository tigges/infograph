Transcribed notes

Handwriting was fast. This is a best-effort reading, checked against Marshall. Full file: notes/transcription.md.

China

  1. Heartland & Hu Line. Agricultural centre of gravity; the line explains population disparity and how China developed.
  2. Borders & buffers. Buffer zones protect the core. Mongolia, Tibet, Xinjiang around the fertile east.
  3. Navy & sea neighbourhood. “A green-water navy patrols its maritime borders.” Trade routes, resources, influence.

Africa

  1. Natural barriers. Poor harbours, waterfalls, north–south split. Contrast the Rhine. Trade routes ✕.
  2. Colonial borders. “Prisoners of the political geography the Europeans made.” Sudan, Somalia. Contrast China’s unified core.
  3. Population growth. “Perhaps Africa’s biggest challenge.” One in four people will be African (p.217).

Comparison

  1. Heartland vs barriers
  2. Chosen buffers vs imposed borders
  3. Navy vs no harbour
